# HackMeridian source snapshot

This is a publication snapshot of Pactlane's completed mainnet implementation, taken from source revision `a568b1909d0cda004ba567ce261ff83c26ecbf21` on 26 October 2026. Publication preparation adds dependency licence copies and attribution; it does not change application or contract code.

Preparatory work before the event included research, a TLSNotary browser launcher/payment plugin, public-response and MPC diagnostics, and an archived Reclaim/Soroban signature experiment. The escrow, payment policy, buyer and seller app, wallet and recovery integration, and completed mainnet trade were developed during the event.

Start with [README](README.md), the [mainnet receipt](docs/release/mainnet-result.md), and [verification evidence](docs/verification.md). The product walkthrough uses a sample trade; the receipt records a real EUR 0.89 payment and 1 Circle USDC settlement on 26 October.
